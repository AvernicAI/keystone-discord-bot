# Pass 08 — P4 AI layer

**Date:** 2026-11-01. **Phase:** P4. **Status:** complete in-repo; live provider
and pgvector ANN gates remain open.

## What changed

- Added `@keystone/module-ai` with a kernel `AiProvider`/`AiStore`/`AiPort`
  contract, a deterministic offline provider, an OpenAI-compatible adapter and
  an explicitly unconfigured provider.
- Added per-guild monthly dollar and request caps in `AiService`, enforced before
  every provider call. Usage is aggregate counters only.
- Added server-doc chunking, embeddings and exact-cosine retrieval as the
  portable reference index. No member message is persisted by the AI module.
- Added `/ai` (status, ask, chat, docs-add/list/remove, deflect, explain, summary,
  budget) and `/ticket summary`. The ledger is 10/100.
- Added ticket deflection (human hand-off when no doc matches), mention-only
  persona chat, advisory classification, moderation explanations that leave the
  case unchanged, and thread summaries.
- Added migration 0005 (`ai_documents`, `ai_usage`), `DrizzleAiStore`, P1-style
  runtime wiring, and the mention event in the bot app layer.
- Added 20 P4 tests (service, provider, command, PGlite) and the 1M-vector
  research harness.
- Wrote the AI Spec/Status/Teaching, ADR-0010 and the pgvector research note.

## What we tried and rejected

- **Direct provider calls in commands.** Rejected: duplicated caps, keys and
  retention decisions.
- **Prompt-level budget.** Rejected: not an enforcement boundary.
- **Per-user cap as the primary limit.** Rejected: a raid can spend the guild's
  budget across users.
- **Prompt/completion retention for debugging.** Rejected: violates the zero
  retention exit and creates a breach liability.
- **Autonomous AI moderation.** Rejected: unreviewable and outside the case
  model.
- **Calling the reference index “pgvector”.** Rejected: it is exact cosine over
  portable JSONB vectors; the extension/ANN benchmark is a separate open gate.
- **Bundling a local model.** Rejected: size, hardware and licence burden.
- **A new root per AI feature.** Rejected: one `/ai` hub plus `/ticket summary`.
