# Pass 07 — P3 completion

## What changed

- Added birthday records and timezone-aware due-day announcements with a
  per-year idempotency marker.
- Added suggestions with one vote per user, up/down buttons, approval/rejection
  and immutable decision status.
- Added select-menu reaction roles (multi-select and multiple managed roles)
  plus the kernel `SelectComponent`, adapter translation and server-side
  reconciliation.
- Added per-channel starboard overrides with independent thresholds and
  destination channels.
- Added delayed autorole records processed by the scheduler tick.
- Added sticky messages with duplicate-event protection and scoped deletion of
  the previous sticky.
- Added seasonal XP reset: profiles are archived to `community_records` under a
  season key inside one transaction, then the `levels` projection is cleared.
- Extended `/rank` with `season` and `/tools` with birthdays, suggestions,
  sticky, starboard override and select-role paths. The ledger remains 9/100.
- Added ADR-0009 and updated the module records.

## What we tried and rejected

- **A separate season store.** Rejected: one transactional `resetLevels` method
  on the existing store is smaller and testable.
- **Deleting levels without an archive.** Rejected: the matrix says archive,
  don’t destroy, and a reset must be explainable.
- **One button per role.** Rejected: Discord’s select menu is the platform-native
  multi-select and costs one row.
- **Editing roles in the interaction handler.** Rejected: the handler forwards
  values; the service checks panel membership and current roles.
- **A `/roles` or `/season` root.** Rejected: `/tools` and `/rank` already own
  these mental models and the command budget is a hard limit.
- **Marking live gateway behavior verified.** Rejected: no token or staging guild
  exists; the adapter remains typechecked, not observed.
