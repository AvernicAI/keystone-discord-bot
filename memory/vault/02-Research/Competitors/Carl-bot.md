# Carl-bot

**Position:** reaction roles, embeds and logging — with genuinely zero leveling.

## Pricing (2026)

Patreon, entry ~£6.50/mo, tier ladder reported at $7.99 / $12.99 / $16.99 for
1 / 2 / 3 servers. Premium slots are **transferable** between servers, which is
the most sensible pricing mechanic in the category.

## What is free vs paid

| Free | Paid |
| --- | --- |
| Reaction roles (250) | 1,000 reaction roles, timed reaction roles |
| 5 YouTube / 2 Twitch feeds | 20 / 5 feeds |
| 100 webhook log entries | 500 entries, separate farewell channel |
| Basic embeds, tags, starboard | Custom embed colour/image, embed scripting |
| Moderation (basic) | Levels, sticky messages, auto-purge, voice-role links, Drama Watcher |

**There is no leveling system at all** on free — that is the gap that forces
admins to keep MEE6 alongside it.

## What they do well

- **Reaction roles are the best in class.** 250 free is more than most paid tiers.
- **Transferable premium** follows the server you choose, not your account.
- **Drama Watcher**: deferred punishment to a review channel with reaction voting.
  Nearly unique, and exactly the human-in-the-loop idea we are building on.
- **Embed scripting** with variables, for power users.

## Where it is weak

- No leveling, and only basic moderation.
- Command-heavy configuration; the dashboard learning curve is steep.
- Logging retention capped on free, so the audit trail expires.
- Community reports of outages and slow support (second-hand, unverified).

## What we take

- Transferable entitlements as a pricing principle.
- Drama Watcher as the seed of our review queue and appeals flow.
- Embed scripting, but as structured data with a validator, not a template language.

## What we do differently

- Leveling ships in the same free bot.
- Logs are database rows, not capped webhook entries.
