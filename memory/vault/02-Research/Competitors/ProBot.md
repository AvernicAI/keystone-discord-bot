# ProBot

**Position:** the generalist that actually sells anti-raid.

## Pricing (2026)

Two tiers, reported at $5/mo and $10/mo per server (annual ~50% off). Tier 1 adds
protection and temporary invite auto-role; Tier 2 adds anti-raid and a
personalised bot identity (name, avatar, status).

The official site returns 403 to scrapers; figures are from documentation and
third-party coverage, so re-verify.

## What is free vs paid

| Free | Paid |
| --- | --- |
| Moderation commands | Tier 1: protection, temporary invite auto-role |
| Automod, logging | Tier 2: anti-raid, custom bot identity, exclusive commands |
| Embeds, reaction roles, custom commands | Higher limits |
| Welcome with images, stat channels | — |

Notably: **leveling is not a headline feature**, and there is no music.

## What they do well

- **Anti-raid in a generalist.** The only mainstream all-in-one that sells it,
  which validates our P2 ordering.
- **Custom bot identity** as an upsell people actually want.
- Simple two-tier pricing.

## Where it is weak

- Anti-raid behind the top tier, and separate from the rest of the security model.
- Personalised bot means re-inviting and re-creating components (same migration
  tax as Dyno).
- Feature depth is shallow across the board; it is a second bot, not the only one.

## What we take

- Anti-raid belongs in the generalist — but free, and sharing the case file.
- Custom identity as a hosting convenience, not a feature gate.

## What we do differently

- Security is P2, before community features, so the case model is right from the
  start.
- One bot, one identity, no re-invite to unlock a tier.
