# Others worth knowing

## Ticket Tool — the dashboard to beat

Free: unlimited tickets, panels, transcripts, knowledge base, 10 AI actions.
Community $5, Pro $12, Team $49, Enterprise from $199. Per server; prices include
VAT.

Free-tier catches: transcripts and analytics auto-delete after **30 days**, free
panels carry a "Powered by" footer, and transcripts cannot be exported to your own
storage. Pro adds REST API, webhook/flow nodes, AI auto-answering, SLA analytics.

**Take:** thread-based tickets (dodges the 50-channel category cap), a live flow
builder, and a generous free tier. **Avoid:** expiring audit trails.

## Wick — security-first, heat-based automod

Premium $5/mo, VIP $20/mo (12 servers, custom branding). Anti-nuke, panic mode,
heat-based automod, quarantine and restore are free; "advanced" versions are paid.
Smart Backups is genuinely paywalled.

**Take:** one-click security setup, panic mode, heat decay instead of binary
thresholds. **Avoid:** restore that admits it "lacks a lot of information".

## Security Bot — depth sold as premium

Core free; Premium adds anti-mention, anti-vanity, anti-rename, anti-emoji-delete,
anti-ghost-ping, score-based anti-raid, configurable verification. Premium+
$8.50/mo adds custom identity.

**Take:** the checklist of things raiders actually do. It is our P2 test matrix.

## Beemo — anti-raid free, everything else paid

Anti-raid free; Max $4.99/mo adds moderation commands, log lookup, message
archives, XP and consumable credits.

**Take:** free anti-raid as a growth strategy. **Avoid:** "Honey credits".

## Xenon — backups, honestly scoped

Free: structure only. Premium 2 $10.99/mo: 100 backups, messages, threads, bans,
role assignments, sync across servers.

**Take:** backup cadence as a product. **Avoid:** paywalling message history,
which is the part admins actually need after an incident.

## GalaxyBot — member-count pricing

Plus from €4.45/mo scaled by members, limited to 12 EU countries. Sells custom
domain, application-page blocks, public API and webhooks.

**Take:** the public API + webhook story. **Avoid:** member-count pricing, which
punishes exactly the communities that need help.

## Arcane — simplest premium story

$7/mo unlocks all plugins; free tier shows ads on the site. Embed scripting and
stat channels are premium.

**Take:** "one price, all plugins" clarity. **Avoid:** ads on the free tier.

## Tatsu — the economy toy

Leveling free for up to 10 reward roles; 2,000+ economy items, largely global.
Premium via Patreon for more slots.

**Take:** role rewards as the point of leveling, not a leaderboard. **Avoid:** a
global economy that ignores server context and is trivially gamed.

## Bleed — command-first anti-nuke

Per-permission watch lists, per-module thresholds, vanity protection, bot-add
blocking. Reported ~$11.95/mo. Configuration through modals, not a dashboard.

**Take:** the granularity of its anti-nuke model. **Avoid:** making novices learn
a command language to turn on protection.

## Shapes — the cautionary tale

Moved off Discord entirely after a mass bot termination in May 2025. Proves that an
AI companion product built on another platform's tolerance has no floor under it.

**Lesson:** AI features must be opt-in, metered, and removable without breaking
the bot.
